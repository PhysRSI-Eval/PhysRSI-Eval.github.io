Physical Barrel Hypothesis

Different aspects of improvement may have different capability bottlenecks: spatial reasoning for starting well, perception for interpreting failures and recovering from them. Strong coding cannot always compensate for a weakness elsewhere.

Public capability and physical RSI separate differently

Across the four GPT systems, mean public benchmark scores span 24 points; the physical profile mean spans 59.5 points. GPT-5.6 Sol stays near zero cumulative success for 16 rounds.

What can early physical feedback predict?

A Weibull template fitted to Astra, calibrated on GPT-6.1 Sol's K1–K5, forecasts K6–K17 with mean absolute error 0.019, versus 0.112 for holding the last observed score.

Can experience help on a different task?

Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 encode control in self-written tools that generate over 96% of later-round actions; Opus reuses the broadest toolbox (lineage reuse 0.347 vs 0.167 for Sonnet and 0.086 for GPT-6 Sol).
